Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Hantavirus Case Tracker is an informational app that displays public outbreak case counts and news links from official or publicly available sources.

Data Collection

Hantavirus Case Tracker does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, health information, contacts, photos, or other personal data.

Network Requests

The app makes network requests to load the public case-count feed and news/source links. These requests may be processed by GitHub Pages, news providers, public health source websites, and Apple's system frameworks. Those services may receive standard technical information such as IP address, device type, browser or app request metadata, and request time.

Analytics and Tracking

Hantavirus Case Tracker does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Medical Disclaimer

This independent app is informational only and is not affiliated with WHO, ECDC, CDC, or any public health agency. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, quarantine, travel, or emergency medical guidance. Follow local public health authority instructions and consult a qualified medical professional for health concerns.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page.
