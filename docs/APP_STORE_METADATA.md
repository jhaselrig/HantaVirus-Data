## Promotional Text

Track reported hantavirus case counts, source links, and related updates from public health sources.

## Description

Hantavirus Tracker is a focused outbreak-tracking app for reported hantavirus case counts and related public health updates.

The app shows a verified case snapshot with total, confirmed, probable, suspected, and death counts, plus the source and data date for the current tracked outbreak. It also includes related news links and a methodology screen so users can see where the numbers come from.

Hantavirus Tracker is informational only. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, quarantine, travel, or emergency medical guidance. Always follow local public health authority instructions and consult a qualified medical professional for health concerns.

## Review Notes

Hantavirus Tracker displays public outbreak case counts from official public health sources and links directly to the current source inside the app. The app is informational only and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, quarantine, travel, or emergency guidance.

The live case-count feed is hosted at:
https://jhaselrig.github.io/HantaVirus-Data/current-outbreak.json

The app does not require login, does not collect personal information, does not use location services, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## App Privacy

Data collection: None.

The app does not collect user data. It makes network requests to load the public case-count feed and external source/news links.
