Hantavirus Tracker Support

Hantavirus Tracker displays public case counts and source links for the current tracked outbreak.

Current Data Feed

The live case-count feed is available at current-outbreak.json.

Data Sources

Case counts are sourced from linked public health updates shown in the app. News links are informational and are not used as authoritative case-count sources.

Affiliation

Hantavirus Tracker is an independent app and is not affiliated with WHO, ECDC, CDC, or any public health agency.

Medical Disclaimer

This app does not provide diagnosis, treatment, quarantine, travel, or emergency medical guidance. For symptoms, exposure concerns, or urgent questions, contact a qualified medical professional or local public health authority.
